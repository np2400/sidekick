Sidekick privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Sidekick is a personal AI assistant on Telegram, built and run by Neel Parekh. It is used by its owner and a few people the owner invites with a one-time link; there is no public sign-up.

Invited users

  • Each user's reminders, tasks, recent messages and pending approvals are kept separately and only that user can see or change them through the bot.
  • Invited users don't get access to the owner's Google Calendar, Gmail or other connected accounts.
  • Invited users can connect their own Google account. /connect asks Google only for calendar access (read events, and create events you approve) plus your email address to show which account is connected. /connect gmail also asks for read-only Gmail access: Sidekick searches and reads your email only when you ask, and can never send, change or delete it. Emails you ask about are sent to the AI model writing the answer (see "How it is used").
  • Your Google sign-in token is stored encrypted, with the key kept outside the database and its backups. The owner runs the server, so this protects against a leaked database or backup, not against the server itself. Send /disconnect to revoke access, or remove Sidekick at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The owner runs the server, so stored data is technically within the owner's reach; Sidekick has no feature for reading another user's messages.

What Sidekick accesses

  • Telegram messages you send to the Sidekick bot, and your Telegram user id.
  • Google Calendar (if connected): reads events when you ask about your schedule, and creates an event only after you approve it.
  • Gmail (if connected; for invited users only with /connect gmail): read-only. Sidekick searches and reads messages only when you ask. It cannot send, change or delete email.
  • Web search: when a question needs current information, the search query is sent to Tavily, a search service, which returns public web results.
  • Photos you send the bot: passed to an AI model that can read images (currently Google Gemini, with Qwen on Groq as a backup) to answer you. Sidekick does not store photos.

How it is used

Only to answer your requests. To write a reply, the text of your request and any calendar or email content Sidekick read for it are sent to the AI model provider generating the reply (currently Groq, with Google Gemini as a fallback, and Gemini first for photos). Sidekick uses Gemini's free tier, where Google may use submitted content to improve its products. Sidekick does not sell data, show ads, or use it for anything else.

What is stored, and where

  • On Sidekick's server (Amazon Web Services, United States): your reminders, tasks, pending approvals, a short history of actions taken, and the Google sign-in token. The database is backed up to Amazon S3, encrypted at rest.
  • Server logs and the saved request traces (kept 90 days) record metadata only (timings, which models and tools ran), never message text, photos or email content.
  • Recent messages: your last messages and Sidekick's replies are kept for up to 7 days so it can follow the conversation (only the last two hours are used). A reply may summarize an email or describe a photo; photos themselves are never stored. Send /forget to delete them at any time.
  • Settings you choose (timezone, morning-brief time) and a daily count of your requests, used for a fair-use limit. The count is a number only, not what you asked.
  • Email and calendar content is otherwise not stored; it is read when needed to answer a request.

Google user data

Sidekick's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

  • /export sends you a file with everything Sidekick has stored about you (sign-in tokens excepted).
  • /delete_me erases all of it and revokes Google access. Copies in the encrypted backups are overwritten within 7 days.
  • /forget clears just the recent conversation; /disconnect removes Google access.
  • Disconnect Google at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The owner can end an invited user's access; their data then stays until they or the owner delete it.
  • Questions or requests: use the contact below.
