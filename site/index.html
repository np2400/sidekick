Sidekick

A personal AI assistant you text on Telegram. It answers questions about your day, keeps reminders and a task list, and can read your Google Calendar and Gmail when you connect them. It asks for approval before it creates anything outside its own database.

Sidekick is a personal project by Neel Parekh. Access is by invitation only; there is no public sign-up.
